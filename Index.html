Superman's Origins

Superman, also known as Clark Kent or Kal-El, is a fictional superhero from the planet Krypton. He was sent to Earth as a baby and raised by the Kent family.

Superpowers

  • Super Strength: Superman can lift and move objects of incredible size and weight.
  • Flight: He defies gravity and can fly at super speeds.
  • Heat Vision: His eyes emit powerful beams of heat which can vary in size, power, and perform various effects.
  • Super Speed: He can move and react faster than any human.
  • Invulnerability: His body is nearly indestructible.
